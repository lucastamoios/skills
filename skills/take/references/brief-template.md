# Issue brief: <ticket or work title>

## What should change and why

Open with one short paragraph or three short bullets that explain what the work
is about, what will change for the affected user compared with today, and why it
matters in business terms. This overview must stand alone when the coordinator
relays it to the user before questions or technical details. Label uncertain scope
and do not invent business value for a technical task.

Describe material changes in prose under descriptive subsections as needed.
Use stable change IDs. State whether a rule is being restored, changed, or
preserved. Mark proposals and unresolved choices explicitly. Use sections and
subsections rather than tables throughout the brief.

## Issue and current workflow

- Identify the issue, affected actor, goal, and significance.
- Explain one concrete example of the current workflow and its limitation.
- Distinguish the reported problem from suggested solutions and existing work.

## Reusable concepts and established rules

Explain the concepts and their relationships in short paragraphs. Link established
definitions and sources, and distinguish established rules from uncertain meaning.
Include only concepts needed to understand this work.

## Evidence and uncertainties

Record material claims with their sources and limits. Distinguish observed,
reported, inferred, and unknown behavior. Record conflicts, unavailable sources,
and tentative scope boundaries.

## Decisions and recommendations

Repeat the following structure for each material decision. Follow the skill's
decision guidance, and make each section understandable without prior discussion.

### <Descriptive decision title> (<stable ID>)

#### Where this applies

Explain the workflow location, affected actor, and owning issue. Walk through a
concrete example, labeling illustrative details as such.

#### What is established and what remains uncertain

Give the relevant evidence and explain the exact gap or conflict.

#### Recommendation and why

Describe the proposed behavior, its business consequence, and material tradeoffs
or alternatives. Show what happens to the example under the recommendation.

#### Confidence and remaining decision

State the degree of confidence and its basis, separately from whether the behavior
is approved. Identify the precise choice, decision owner, and dependent work.

State explicitly when no clarification is needed. Keep confirmed answers with
their sources when resuming, without presenting them again as open decisions.

## Handoff

- State the recommended next stage and why it is appropriate.
- Identify work that can proceed and work waiting on answers.
- Link reusable artifacts and confirmed decisions for the next worker.
- State what remains outside the current scope.

## Related task

- Identify the task path, or record that this work is explicitly untracked.
- Propose a concise task update with the brief link, change summary, rationale,
  open question IDs and owners, and next action.
