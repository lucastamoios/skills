# Editorial Style

Produce a transcript that could appear in a professionally edited interview or book. Preserve the speaker's ideas, emotional force, and recognizable voice. Remove the accidental debris of speaking and transcription.

## Default register

- Use standard spelling, agreement, capitalization, and punctuation.
- Prefer complete, natural sentences. Split long run-on speech at genuine idea boundaries.
- Normalize phonetic reductions such as `tô`, `tá`, and `pra` to `estou`, `está`, and `para` when this improves the sentence.
- Keep characteristic vocabulary such as `cara`, `velho`, `viu`, or `umas paradas` only when it acts as a meaningful vocative, emphasis, or voice marker. Remove it when it is repeated filler.
- Preserve technical English terms when they are the natural vocabulary of the speaker.

## Coarse language and interjections

- Remove an isolated profanity or coarse interjection when it adds no meaning.
- When it carries emphasis or surprise, replace it with a natural non-vulgar equivalent that preserves the force, such as `carai` to `caramba`.
- Preserve coarse wording only when the exact wording is itself substantive, such as a quoted insult, a discussion about the expression, or an emotional reaction whose intensity cannot be preserved otherwise.
- Do not replace every informal expression with formal prose. The result must still sound like the same person.

## Fidelity boundary

Surface editing may change words, sentence boundaries, and punctuation. It must not remove or invent a proposition. Preserve names, actors, objects, reasons, conditions, negations, uncertainty, decisions, commitments, and changes of position.

Before accepting each rewritten turn, check that every source idea has a visible clause in the result. Then read the result aloud. It must sound conversational but no longer sound like raw automatic transcription.

## Examples

> Raw: Carai, o cara fez um prensado. Caramba, aí você vai gerar um artefato com esse resultado aí, né?
>
> Cleaned: Caramba, o cara fez um prensado. Aí você vai gerar um artefato com esse resultado, não é?

> Raw: O o serviço já tá rodando, mas eu ainda não conferi se a fila acabou.
>
> Cleaned: O serviço já está rodando, mas eu ainda não conferi se a fila acabou.
